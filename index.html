About

I am a Systems and Generative AI Engineer at Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), architecting multi-agent systems and distributed intelligence platforms for enterprise observability and IT Operations Management (ITOM).

At HPE, I am the primary contributor to our Agentic Observability Harness (comparable in scope to AWS DevOps Agent) — an enterprise platform orchestrating 600+ tools via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to automate Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and incident remediation across hundreds of enterprise tenants.

I hold a Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering (focused on Machine Learning and Signal Processing) from the University of Wisconsin–Madison. Prior to HPE, I conducted computational medical imaging research at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), developing novel complex-valued neural network architectures resulting in two first-author publications in IEEE Transactions on Computational Imaging and The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
