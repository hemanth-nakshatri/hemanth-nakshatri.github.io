About

A hybrid systems engineer combining production distributed platforms with peer-reviewed machine learning research.

I am a Cloud Engineer II at Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) in San Jose, California. My engineering focus is on autonomous multi-agent systems and high-throughput observability platforms capable of operating across petabyte-scale distributed telemetry.

At HPE, I am the lead contributor to the Agentic Observability Harness. The system orchestrates over 600 tools via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) without context saturation, enabling autonomous multi-hypothesis Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and cloud resource orchestration across hybrid cloud environments.

I hold a Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison (Machine Learning and High-Performance Computing). Previously, I was a Project Associate at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) under Prof. Jaya Prakash, authoring first-author publications in IEEE Transactions on Computational Imaging and The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
